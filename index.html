About Lay's

EVERY BITE BRINGS A SMILE.

Lay's is a snack brand loved around the world, known for golden, crispy potato chips and a flavour for every mood. Whether it's a quiet moment on your own or a big get-together with friends, Lay's is made to be opened, shared and enjoyed.

This website is an independent concept demo that imagines a premium online snack shop — a place to browse flavours, build a snack box and share the joy of a good crunch.
